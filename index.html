SafeDeal's Protection Fee Policy

Why we hold funds in escrow, and how the fee works.

Why we hold your buyer's payment

SafeDeal exists to make your customers trust you a little faster, especially the ones who only know your business from an Instagram post or a WhatsApp forward. When a buyer pays through a SafeDeal Link, we hold their money in escrow first. We only release it to you once they've confirmed their order actually arrived. That promise is what makes a stranger comfortable paying you before they've met you.

The protection fee

Holding and releasing funds safely takes work on our end, so we charge a protection fee of 3% of the total order value on every order. Your buyer never sees this fee. They only ever see the price you've listed.

You decide how to handle it

Some vendors raise their prices slightly to cover the fee. Others leave their prices exactly as they are and let SafeDeal deduct the fee from what they set. Both are completely fine, it's entirely your call, and you can change your approach any time.

A floor and a ceiling

The protection fee is never less than ₦500 or more than ₦5,000 on a single order, no matter how small or large the sale is.

Terms and Conditions

Please read before agreeing.

1. About SafeDeal

SafeDeal is a payment protection service for social commerce in Nigeria. We sit between a vendor and a buyer: the vendor creates a SafeDeal Link for a product, the buyer pays into escrow instead of sending money directly to a stranger, and funds are only released to the vendor once the buyer confirms the order has been delivered.

2. Accounts and Verification

To create a SafeDeal Link, vendors must complete our verification process, which includes confirming your identity and business details. Until you're verified, you can explore SafeDeal but cannot create a live payment link. We may ask for more information at any time to keep our platform trustworthy, and we may suspend or decline to verify an account that provides false or misleading information.

3. How Escrow Works

When a buyer pays through a SafeDeal Link, the funds are held by SafeDeal, not the vendor, until the buyer confirms delivery or five days pass without a dispute being raised, whichever happens first. Once released, funds belong to the vendor, minus the protection fee described below.

4. Protection Fee

SafeDeal charges vendors a protection fee of 3% of the total order value on every completed order, with a minimum fee of ₦500 and a maximum fee of ₦5,000 per order. This fee is deducted from the amount released to the vendor and is never added to or shown as part of the buyer's total.

5. Disputes

If a buyer believes an order was not delivered, was damaged, or does not match what was described, they may raise a dispute before confirming delivery. SafeDeal will review evidence from both sides and decide how funds are released. Filing knowingly false disputes, or shipping items that do not match what was listed, may result in account suspension.

6. Prohibited Use

You may not use SafeDeal to sell illegal goods or services, to launder money, to impersonate another business or person, or to circumvent escrow by asking a buyer to pay you directly outside a SafeDeal Link. We may suspend or close accounts that violate this section without notice.

7. Account Status

Vendor accounts may be Awaiting Verification, Verified, Under Review, or Suspended at any time, and your ability to create SafeDeal Links depends on that status. We'll always tell you why your status changed and, where applicable, how to resolve it.

8. Changes to These Terms

We may update these terms as SafeDeal grows. If we make a material change, we'll let you know before it takes effect. Continuing to use SafeDeal after a change means you accept the update.

9. Limitation of Liability

SafeDeal facilitates payments and holds funds in escrow, but we are not a party to the underlying sale between a buyer and vendor. We are not responsible for the quality, safety, or legality of items sold through SafeDeal Links, though we will always act in good faith when resolving disputes.

10. Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to support@safedeal.ng.

Data Privacy Policy

Please read before agreeing.

1. What This Policy Covers

This policy explains what information SafeDeal collects from vendors and buyers, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

2. Information We Collect

Account information: your name, email address, phone number, and password.

Vendor verification information: your business name, business address, and any identity or business documents you provide during verification.

Transaction information: products, prices, delivery details, payment amounts, and escrow status for each SafeDeal Link you create or use.

Communications: messages you send us for support, and evidence you submit as part of a dispute.

Device and usage information: basic technical data like browser type and how you interact with SafeDeal, used to keep the platform working properly.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to run the escrow service, verify vendor identities, process and release payments, resolve disputes fairly, prevent fraud, and communicate with you about your account and transactions. We do not use your information to sell you unrelated products or share it with advertisers.

4. What We Share, and With Whom

A vendor and buyer in the same transaction can see the information relevant to that transaction, for example a buyer sees the vendor's business name and verification badge, and a vendor sees the buyer's name and delivery details, but nothing more. We share verification information with the partners we use to confirm identity and business details, and we may share information with regulators or law enforcement where we're legally required to. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

5. How Long We Keep Information

We keep account and transaction information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period after that to meet our legal, tax, and dispute-resolution obligations.

6. Your Choices

You can review and update most of your account information from your profile at any time. Business name and business address are locked once verification is complete to protect the integrity of your verified badge, but you can request a change through your profile and our team will review it. You can also contact us to ask what information we hold about you.

7. Keeping Your Information Safe

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including restricting who can access verification documents and payment details. No system is perfectly secure, but we take this responsibility seriously.

8. Children's Privacy

SafeDeal is intended for adults running or supporting a business or making a purchase. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make a material change to this policy, we'll let you know before it takes effect.

10. Contact

Questions about your privacy can be sent to privacy@safedeal.ng.
